# Flag assets: sources, variants and licenses

Verified and downloaded from the linked Wikimedia Commons file pages on **15 September 2026**. These are local vector assets, not remotely loaded images. The source file's stripe order, colors, proportions, chevrons, circles and flowers are retained.

`src/flags.ts` is the application catalog. `flags/sources.json` records source URLs, license URLs, original and bundled SHA-256 hashes, viewBoxes and changes for reproducibility.

## Reuse

The flags retain their individual licenses independently of the application code. Keep this notice and the source/author information when redistributing these assets. The two CC BY-SA 4.0 assets require credit, a license link and an indication of changes; adaptations remain under CC BY-SA 4.0. Animated/deformed versions of those flags are adaptations: retain their attribution and license when sharing them.

- [CC0 1.0 dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- [CC BY-SA 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- [Public-domain geometric works](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Template:PD-shape)
- [Copyrighted free use permission](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Template:Copyrighted_free_use)

All files underwent nonvisual cleanup: editor metadata and comments were removed, XML was normalized, and percentage-only dimensions were resolved from the original viewBox. No flag's drawing geometry was redesigned. Attribution is retained below even where the source does not require it.

## Source details

- **Progress Pride:** the current [official Daniel Quasar page](https://progress.gay/) explicitly releases the flag design under CC0 for commercial and noncommercial use. Older descriptions of a noncommercial-only license are superseded by that current notice. The Commons SVG by Paul2520 is also CC0.
- **Intersex-inclusive Progress Pride:** Commons identifies Valentino Vecchietti's 2021 design and Nikki's SVG, released under CC0. Both the chevrons and intersex circle are retained.
- **Sapphic:** the bundled asset is the public-domain Commons violet-flower variant credited to Cayla / lexbeux-moved, matching the supplied `Sapphic_Flag_alternate_with_violet.svg`. The artist credit is preserved; no private profile picture is included.
- **Achillean:** Commons `MLM flag.svg` is the complete blue/cream flag with its green carnation, released under CC0. Commons does not identify the original flag designer; Kautr uploaded the earlier file and Intervex supplied the current vector version. The catalog does not attribute the design to an invented author.
- **Gay men:** the linked source's current licensing section identifies the seven-stripe gayflagblog design as public domain. Its older structured-data field still mentions CC BY-SA 4.0; the catalog follows the current file-page licensing statement and retains author credit.
- **Demiromantic and omnisexual:** MarcoSnail and pastelroswell respectively; CC BY-SA 4.0. The local SVG cleanup is declared above and these files remain under that license.

## Adding a flag

Choose a documented variant and verify the specific source file's license. Add the local, self-contained SVG and its entry to `src/flags.ts`; update this notice and `flags/sources.json`. Keep symbols intact, preserve the viewBox, and avoid scripts, external images, linked fonts or external CSS. Display thumbnails with `object-fit: contain` and use the complete flag image in the video renderer.
